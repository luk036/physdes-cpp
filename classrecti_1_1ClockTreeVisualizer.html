Detailed Description

Visualizes clock trees in SVG format.

Configured through the nested Builder (Builder pattern) instead of a telescoping constructor. Example:

auto viz = ClockTreeVisualizer::Builder()
.node_radius(10)
.wire_width(3)
.sink_color("#2E7D32")
.build();
